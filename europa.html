Europa


Game details


Europa is an action-adventure indie video game that takes place on the terraformed moon Europa. You play as the android Zee who is on a quest for answers after discovering a journal with missing pages made out to him. Throughout the game you will find pages to complete the journal and figure out what exactly happened on this moon. The game was developed by Novadust Entertainment and published by Future Friends Games.

Graphic design - 10/10


The world of Europa is no less than beautiful. It’s almost as if you’re walking inside a painting. Not only is the sky a work of art, so is the land and the animals. Each world you explore is even more beautiful and breathtaking than the last. Europa took the cake with this one.

Plot - 10/10


The story of Zee and his dead father leaves a beautiful and strong impact on anyone who collects every single page. As you journey through each section of the moon Europa, you discover a part of Zee that makes him pure and innocent; a child unknown to the past of the world. It unveils a story that is both inspiring and touching. It is rightfully paced with barely any flaws. The ending leaves you torn, but in a beautiful way, knowing that you’ve experienced something amazing and have gained great knowledge.

Game genre - 9/10


This would have been a perfect score if it had more action involved with it. The adventure aspect is perfect. With the sketch book feature in the game, it encourages you to wander and explore, looking for the diverse life on Europa. The world building is well laid out and it gives you plenty of room to explore! There isn’t a big action aspect in the game as all you really do is solve very simple puzzles and avoid enemies whom of which you can’t fight against.

Controls - 8.5/10


When first picking up the game, the controls were a little wonky to me and I felt that they were a little harder to correlate with the physics of the game. However, after a few sessions I got used to them and found the best way for me to enjoy the game.

Performance - 8/10


For me playing it on my switch, the performance wasn’t the best. Not only were the graphics sucky, but there were several instances of lagging and the game causing my switch to turn off. Besides that, the other features made it amazing!

Advertisement - 8/10


Super basic. There wasn’t much to put this game out. The best way it got boosted was through articles that explained how it was a perfect game for Studio Ghibli lovers. It still remains a hidden gem.

Player/ fanbase - 9/10


The game has been very well received amongst players and many people love it! I haven’t seen anyone talk bad about the game besides the fact that many believe it is overpriced.

Music - 10/10


As you progress in the game, the music increases with intensity and longing as you’re reaching this huge secret that has been countlessly talked about in your father’s journal. Whether it’s the piano, or the strings, it’s some of the most simplistic beautifully crafted music, just like botw. If there’s any video game soundtrack that should be noted as one of the best, include Europa!

Gameplay - 10/10


The whole point of the game is finding the missing pages in your father’s journal, which turns into a heartfelt story about the love of a son from a father. As you journey through the beautiful lands of Europa, you learn of its beauty and how different it is from earth, such as the capability to fly and glide in the air. Going through the game as an android shows you how beautiful the game is, especially once you find your way home and decipher what actually happened on Europa.

Average score - 9.2/10
