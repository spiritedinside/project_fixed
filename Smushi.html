Smushi Come Home


Game details


Smushi Come Home is an indie open world narrative game about a mushroom named Smushi who gets taken by a bird and thrown far into a forest. In order to get home, Smushi needs to talk to many different people throughout different kinds of forests in order to get home! SCH is the first game created by SomeHumbleOnion and is published under Mooneye studios, which is an indie studio that has released other smaller indie games.

Graphic design - 10/10


The graphics in this game are stellar! Of course it’s not hyper-realistic but that isn’t always needed for a cozy game such as this. The cartoony style is very appealing for a very tiny mushroom that is exploring lots of different forests. The world building is also very genius, as it gives smushi very small items to use; like leaves as gliders and sewing pins as swords. Even when it comes down to lighting aspects, that also works very well with this games aesthetic! The way they portray different mushrooms that are real life mushrooms is a very cool feature of the game! They also love to add little Japanese cultural elements and other earthly elements to boost the surrounding area of the game. The different skins that Smushi can wear are also designed uniquely that make it even more fun and satisfying to collect more skins! I also loved the way they created text bubbles when talking to npc’s.

Plot - 9/10


The plot is very simple and straightforward. All you need to do is find your way home! That makes this small game an open world adventure where you’re capable of talking to several different npc’s that either have fun side quests for you to complete or quests to help you find your way home! Some of the npc’s tend to be quirky or strange but that makes the adventure all the more unique. The whole adventure is a wonderfully wholesome experience that I believe anyone can enjoy!

Game genre - 10/10


This game fits several different genres. To start off, it’s clear that this is an open world game. It takes many aspects of exploration in order to find your way home. After all, you are navigating quite a few forests. Within using this exploration element, you can find many different things to do and many different side quests to complete, which is the whole point of an open world game. That also ties into the adventure genre, as the game greatly fits that genre. It’s also a cozy game! While it is indeed an open world and adventure game, there isn’t too much fighting that you need to do… besides whack some mutated things with sticks that can’t fight back. After a long day, I found this game to be very relaxing and stress free, which perfectly fits the cozy genre. This game is most definitely a hidden gem amongst indies, which is what makes it even more satisfying to play through.

Controls - 9/10


Suuuper simple. There isn’t much that Smushi can do. Your general action button can only be used when you approach objects that indicate an action button. You don’t need to guess on anything! The game doesn’t hide much from the player and it makes things super obvious. Again, going back into that exploration feature, the only way you won’t know about stuff in the game is if you don’t explore!

Performance - 9.5/10


Overall the performance is extremely smooth! There were a few laggy areas in the map but I rarely came across them. Even on a switch, it performs greatly!

Advertisement - 9/10


The game has quite a few launch trailers that received lots of views on each video! The creator loves to keep posting about the game even after its launch, which gives it even more advertisement.

Player/ fanbase - 10/10


From a lot of the reviews I have seen, there are several different cozy gamers that absolutely love the game and recommend it to everyone! There isn’t much of a fandom or fanbase besides the creator making silly videos about his game, but other than that the feedback is amazing!

Music - 10/10


The music aligns greatly with the world you choose to explore. When you dwell in caves, the music is very boomy and echoey as if you were in an actual cave. The main music in each forest is pop-like with happy elements to it, making it more wholesome than it already is! I loved the music so much that I checked out the artist and found some other games they worked on, which I will check out sometime in the future!

Gameplay - 10/10


Words simply can’t express how relieving and nice it was to play a game like this. It’s very warm and welcoming after a looong day. Quite literally the perfect game to wind down with. Not only that, it’s appealing and fun! I think any one of any age would love this game!

Average score - 9.6/10
